Connect your print queue

  1. Find your printer. Search your local network automatically or choose an adapter.
  2. Connect a queue. Use the installed Windows driver. Create a separate queue or switch the existing one.
  3. Print a test page. After that, send documents to the same queue from your Windows apps.

Available in 16 languages. Choose your language in EXE setup or under Settings → General → Language. See what’s new in 1.3.1 ↗

Requires an IPv4 local network and a printer and driver that support RAW TCP/9100.
